<p class="lead" style="margin-top: -8px; max-width: 760px;">
  My research examines how agricultural and natural ecosystems respond to environmental change and how land and resource management influence productivity, greenhouse gas emissions, and sustainability. I combine field measurements, satellite data, GIS, and environmental modeling to study ecosystem processes and develop information that can support agricultural management and environmental decision-making.
</p>

<div class="sec theme" id="model-development">
  <span class="num">1</span>
  <h2>Model development</h2>
  <p class="sub">I develop and evaluate models that use field observations, satellite data, and environmental information to estimate crop growth, ecosystem productivity, and carbon exchange. A major focus is improving estimates of crop development and productivity across different fields, growing seasons, and environmental conditions.</p>
  <h4 class="sublab">Current and ongoing research</h4>
  <div class="cards">
    <article class="c">
      <div class="b"><span class="badge">Current</span><span class="when">Arkansas</span>
        <h3>Monitoring crop growth and productivity</h3>
        <p>I am continuing my research on satellite-based estimates of crop growing-season length and productivity. This work includes mapping field-level planting and harvest dates, improving models of rice productivity, and developing open datasets and code for applications across Arkansas. It builds on my dissertation research and published work on crop phenology and productivity.</p>

<div class="sec theme" id="decision-support">
  <span class="num">2</span>
  <h2>Decision-support tools and policy recommendations</h2>
  <p class="sub">I use spatial analysis and environmental data to identify differences in agricultural performance and understand the factors that may explain them. This research connects model outputs with information on soils, water, climate, and land management to produce maps and datasets that can support agricultural planning, resource management, and policy discussions.</p>
  <h4 class="sublab">Current and ongoing research</h4>
  <div class="cards">
    <article class="c">
      <div class="b"><span class="badge">Current</span><span class="when">Arkansas</span>
        <h3>Mapping agricultural productivity and growing conditions</h3>
        <p>I am developing spatial information on rice productivity and growing-season length to support the assessment of agricultural performance across Arkansas. The work connects satellite-based crop monitoring with field observations and environmental data, with the longer-term aim of supporting decisions about crop management and agricultural planning.</p>
        <div class="tg"><span>Crop productivity</span><span>Growing conditions</span><span>Spatial analysis</span></div>
      </div>
    </article>
  </div>

<div class="sec theme" id="sustainability">
  <span class="num">3</span>
  <h2>Environmental sustainability</h2>
  <p class="sub">I examine the environmental impacts of agricultural production and resource use, with particular attention to greenhouse gas emissions, carbon accounting, and opportunities to improve resource efficiency. My work combines field-based measurements with emissions inventories, spatial analysis, and life-cycle assessment to better understand environmental impacts and inform sustainability planning.</p>
  <h4 class="sublab">Current and ongoing research</h4>
  <div class="cards">
    <article class="c">
      <div class="b"><span class="badge">Current</span><span class="when">University of Arkansas</span>
        <h3>Institutional sustainability and carbon reduction</h3>
        <p>I contribute to institutional carbon footprint analyses covering transportation, food systems, and campus sustainability initiatives. This work brings together information on energy, waste, water, and emissions to support sustainability reporting and decarbonization planning.</p>
        <div class="tg"><span>Carbon accounting</span><span>Emissions reporting</span><span>Decarbonization</span></div>
      </div>
    </article>
    <article class="c">
      <div class="b"><span class="badge">Current</span><span class="when">Arkansas</span>
        <h3>Greenhouse gas emissions from agricultural systems</h3>
        <p>My research examines methane and carbon dioxide exchange in rice fields under different water-management practices and during fallow periods. This work contributes to understanding agricultural greenhouse gas emissions and improving assessments of the environmental impacts of rice production.</p>
