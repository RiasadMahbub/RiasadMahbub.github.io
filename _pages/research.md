<p class="lead" style="margin-top:-8px">Research with the Runkle and Reba labs at the University of Arkansas Department of Biological and Agricultural Engineering, centered on rice agroecosystems in the Lower Mississippi Delta.</p>

<div class="lines" style="margin:40px 0 8px">
  <div class="line"><h3>Carbon flux modeling</h3><p>Gross primary productivity of rice with the Vegetation Photosynthesis Model, calibrated on flux towers.</p></div>
  <div class="line"><h3>Crop phenology</h3><p>Satellite time series and machine learning to detect planting and harvest dates at field scale.</p></div>
  <div class="line"><h3>Greenhouse gases</h3><p>Methane and CO₂ fluxes under contrasting water management and fallow-season practices.</p></div>
</div>
